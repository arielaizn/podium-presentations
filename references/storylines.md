# Storylines

Pick the frame before picking slides. A deck is an argument; each slide is one step of it.

## 1. Problem → Answer → Proof → Plan → Ask (default for marketing and sales)
1. Cover — the promise in one line (cover)
2. The tension — what is broken or at stake (statement, often with a photo)
3. Why it matters — numbers or a customer voice (kpi / chart / quote)
4. The answer — the idea in one sentence (section or statement)
5. How it works — 3-5 parts (cards / process)
6. Proof — results, logos, testimonials, before/after (kpi / chart / comparison / quote)
7. Plan — what happens when (timeline / agenda)
8. Who — the people behind it (team / image-text)
9. Offer — price or options (pricing / kpi)
10. Ask — one decision, one next step (closing)

## 2. SCQA (internal updates, board/leadership)
Situation (kpi) → Complication (chart with highlight) → Question (statement) → Answer (section + cards/process) → Next steps (timeline) → Decision needed (closing).

## 3. Pitch arc (investors, partners)
Cover → Problem → Why now → Solution → Product (image-text/gallery) → Traction (chart/kpi) → Market → Business model (pricing/cards) → Team → Ask.

## 4. Lesson / webinar
Cover → What you'll be able to do (cards) → Agenda → per module: section → concept (statement / image-text) → steps (process) → example (quote / gallery) → Recap (comparison or cards) → Homework / CTA (closing).

## 5. Launch / announcement
Teaser statement → What it is (image-text) → 3 reasons (cards) → How it works (process) → Proof (quote/kpi) → Availability & price (pricing) → Closing.

## Rules of thumb
- One idea per slide; the title states the takeaway ("Demos convert, top of funnel is thin"), not the topic ("Pipeline").
- Every number needs a comparison (vs. last quarter, vs. target, vs. competitor) or it means nothing.
- The ask on the last slide must be answerable with yes/no or a date.
- Section slides every 4-6 content slides in decks over 12 slides.
- Put detail in speaker notes, not on the slide, when density is minimal or light.
